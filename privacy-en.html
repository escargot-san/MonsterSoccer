Privacy Policy

Established: November 22, 2026

@さん (the "Developer") sets out this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") for the handling of user information in the smartphone game app "Yokai Soccer" (the "App").

This is a translation for reference. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information we collect

All users

  • Owner ID (an identifier the App creates for each device)
  • Game data (teams, cards, Spirit Stone and Soul balances, match, league and Totocalcio records, story progress, etc.)
  • Age group (for the monthly spending limit on paid items; we do not collect your date of birth)
  • Purchase records for paid items (Google Play order ID, date and time, item, amount). The Developer does not receive payment details such as credit card numbers
  • The content of inquiries and requests and a reply address (if entered), your device model, Android version and App version
  • Records of communication with our server (IP address, date and time, pages accessed, etc.)

Users who set up data transfer with a Google account

  • Your Google account identifier (stored in a form that cannot be reversed) and email address (stored partly masked). We do not receive your Google account password
  • Game data we keep so you can move to a new device

2. Android permissions

The App uses Android permissions for the following purposes.

  • Internet: to communicate with our server (gacha, online league, purchase checks, notices, inquiries, data transfer, etc.) and to show ads
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth scan, advertise and connect, nearby Wi-Fi devices, viewing and changing Wi-Fi state): to find and connect directly to a nearby smartphone for local matches
  • Location (precise and approximate): Android (Google's Nearby Connections) requires this permission to find nearby smartphones for local matches. The App does not obtain, store or send your location by GPS or any other means
  • Google Play billing: to buy paid items
  • Advertising ID: to show ads (see "4. Ads")

The nearby devices and location permissions are requested when you tap "Create a room (home)" or "Join a room (away)" in local match. You can use everything other than local match without granting them. In a local match, your team and tactics data and your own character images are sent directly to the other smartphone (not through our server).

3. Paid items

The App has a paid item, "Spirit Stones". Purchases and payments are handled by Google Play. The Developer receives purchase records to confirm purchases and to manage unused balances (as required by the Japanese Payment Services Act). There is a monthly spending limit based on your age group.

4. Ads

The App uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads (watch an ad to recover stamina). For delivering ads, measuring them and preventing fraud, Google may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and records of ad views and taps directly through the SDK built into the App. The Developer does not receive this information.

  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (rating G)
  • Where the law requires it (such as the EEA and the UK), an ad consent screen is shown first
  • You can delete or reset your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device settings
  • How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the App (game progress, online league, gacha, data transfer)
  • Confirm purchases of paid items, manage unused balances, and handle refunds and cancellations
  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Prevent misuse and deal with violations of the Terms of Use
  • Fix bugs and improve the service

6. Information about creators (designers)

Creator (designer) registration is not available yet. When we start accepting registrations, we will add this section and the information we collect to this Policy.

7. Sharing information with third parties

The App does not provide users' information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes (including selling or renting it).

We do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except:

  • When required by law
  • When necessary to protect a person's life, body or property and it is difficult to obtain your consent
  • When we pass information to the services in "8. External services" as far as needed to run the App (payments, payouts, sending email, etc.)

Information collected by the ad SDK described in 4 is collected directly by Google and is not provided by the Developer.

8. External services (where information is sent)

The App and its server use the following services.

  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): buying paid items and confirming purchases. Sent: purchase information and a converted value of your owner ID
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (Google LLC): showing ads and ad consent. Sent: advertising ID, IP address, device information, etc.
  • Sign in with Google (Google LLC): data transfer setup. Sent: the information needed to confirm the sign-in
  • Google Nearby Connections (Google LLC): connecting devices for local matches. Data is exchanged directly between the devices

Privacy policies of these companies

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Providing information to third parties in other countries

The providers of the services in 8 include companies in other countries (such as the United States and Singapore). These companies have systems in place to protect personal information. You can find information about each country's personal information protection system on the website of Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/kaiseihogohou/#gaikoku ).

10. Security

  • The App's server is located in Japan
  • Access to the server is limited to administrators, and administrators' actions are logged
  • Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, payout details, inquiry content, etc. are stored encrypted on the server
  • Passwords are stored in a form that cannot be reversed
  • Account and purchase data on your device is stored encrypted

11. Retention and deletion

  • Game data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete your account, we delete your game data, the link to your Google account and the backup we keep
  • Purchase and payment records are kept for the period required by law (tax law, the Payment Services Act, etc.; 7 years) and then deleted
  • Inquiry records are kept for 3 years after the inquiry is closed and then deleted
  • Communication records (IP addresses, etc.) are deleted within 90 days

You can delete your account from "Support" → "Delete account" in the App.

12. Requests for disclosure, correction, deletion, etc.

If you would like your information disclosed, corrected, its use stopped or deleted, please contact us from "Support" → "Contact" in the App or at the contact below. After confirming your identity, we will respond in accordance with the law.

13. Use by children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a minor, be sure to get your parent's or guardian's consent before buying paid items.

14. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be changed without notice due to changes in laws or new features of the App. The changed Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

15. Contact

For questions about this Policy, please contact:
