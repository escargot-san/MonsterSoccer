ABOUT

What is Yokai Soccer?

A futsal game where you build a team of yokai and mythical characters and win with tactics.

You don't move the players. As the manager you set the lineup and tactics, and the match plays out automatically with live commentary. It is an app for Android.

FEATURES

How to play

Build a team

Make a team from cards: 5 players (including the GK) and 1 manager. You can also set tactics such as where to keep the ball and the defensive line.

Turn the tide with skills

Every card has skills that trigger with a cut-in during the match. A manager's skill affects the whole team.

Train

Stack the same card to raise its ★ and boost all its stats. Special training lets you raise the stats you like.

Story

Enjoy the conversations as you climb a league full of colorful rival teams.

Online league

Compete for the top spot in the same league as other players' teams.

Local match

Connect directly to a nearby friend's smartphone and play. Two players can also play on one smartphone.

PURCHASE

Purchases

Spirit Stones

A paid item used for the gacha and for card slots. Purchases are paid through Google Play.

Limits by age

  • Under 16: up to 5,000 yen per month
  • 16–19: up to 10,000 yen per month
  • 20 and over: no limit

Recover stamina with ads

Watch an ad to recover stamina (limited number of times per day).

If you are a minor, be sure to get your parent's or guardian's consent before buying.

CREATOR

Creators (designers)

Register characters you drew as cards and put them in the gacha. Part of the sales of your cards is paid to your registered bank account or PayPal (each transfer has a fee of about 750 yen).

To register, you must agree to the Creator Terms and register information for identity verification. See the Privacy Policy for details.
